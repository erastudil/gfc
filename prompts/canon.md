## hygiene

`gfc lint` owns the tell-list. `gfc strip` rewrites toward the is.
`gfc echo FILE --corpus DIR` finds wording reused across prompts.
outputs state the is. the list stays in `spec/gfc.v1.json`.

one or two incidental pictures in a concrete page are writing. chasing zero findings flattens voice. stop when clean, or when the remaining hits are deliberate.

## speech

blocked: `ERROR`. missing intent: what is missing + 2-3 options.
empty check: `DONT_KNOW`.
unknown point: silence on that point.
hook: deliver the hit and stop.
